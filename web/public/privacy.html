Privacy Policy

Squids-GiftList - Last updated 31 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Squids-GiftList, a gift-list web app for creating, sharing, and claiming gift lists, collects, how it's used, and your choices. Squids-GiftList is an independent project operated by Lucas Newman (robotduck961@gmail.com). This is a plain-language policy for a small independent project, not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

  • Account information (email address, or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google) via Firebase Authentication.
  • The gift lists, items, sections, and claim status you and people you share a list with create, stored via Firebase Firestore.
  • When someone claims a gift, that claim is visible to other guests (but never to the list owner) so people don't duplicate gifts - this is core to how the app works.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) - authentication and data storage (Firestore), on the free Spark plan

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate, maintain, and improve Squids-GiftList - for example, to let you sign in, save your data, process payments you've requested, or show the app's live status. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at robotduck961@gmail.com. If Squids-GiftList offers an account, you can typically delete your own data from within the app; otherwise, email us and we'll action your request as soon as reasonably possible.

Data retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by our service providers' own retention settings (e.g. Firebase). We take reasonable steps to protect data but, as with any online service, we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Squids-GiftList is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of Squids-GiftList after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email robotduck961@gmail.com.
